So what's a calango?

A small lizard from the Brazilian sertão.

It lives on burning rocks, moves in fast bursts and never sits still for long. Tiny, loud when it wants to be, built for the heat.

That's the band. Same energy, more drums.

The bateria / interactive

Play the
bateria.

Every row is one section of the band. Every square is one hit. Press play, tap squares, break the groove, then put it back together. This is basically what ensaio feels like.
